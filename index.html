01 / The workflow

Industrial buying,
one clean flow.

IndustrialConnect is built around the real work: requirement → qualification → quotation → buyer selection → production → delivery. The platform helps both sides coordinate; payment is handled directly between buyer and supplier according to their agreed commercial terms.

01 / BUYER

Post RFQ

Describe material, drawing, quantity, delivery location, target date and commercial requirements.

02 / MATCH

Find suppliers

Match the requirement by capability, location, industry, MOQ, lead time and supplier profile.

03 / QUOTE

Compare offers

Receive structured quotations with price, lead time, terms, certificates and notes.

04 / ORDER

Track delivery

Once buyer and supplier agree, record the order and track production and delivery milestones.
